## What this website does
This website is static. It has no accounts, no payments, and no enquiry form at present. It does not set tracking cookies of its own.

## Your documents and history
- We treat your CV, work history and contact details as private.
- We do not feed client data to external AI models, and we do not use it to train any models.
- We do not sell or share your details.
- Ask us to delete your material at any time and we will.

## Hosting
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own policies.
