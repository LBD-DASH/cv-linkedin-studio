## 1. You send your real history
A current CV or a plain list of your roles, dates and responsibilities, and the kind of job you want.

## 2. We tailor and sharpen
We organise your history, tighten the wording and focus it on your target role. We may ask you questions where something is unclear.

## 3. You check everything
You review every draft. Anything that is not accurate gets corrected or removed.

## The rule we keep
We never invent jobs, titles, dates, metrics or skills. We do not promise interviews, job offers or any particular result from applicant tracking software.
